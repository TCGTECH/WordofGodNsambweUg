About Us

Who we are, what we believe, and why we gather.

Our story

Word Of God Redeemed Church began as a small group of believers in Nsambwe, Mukono, meeting to pray and study the Bible together. Today we are a family of all ages who worship together every week.

Why "the Potter's House"?

God told Jeremiah to go to the potter's house, where he watched clay being remade in the potter's hands. That is our picture of church: a place where God reshapes us.

  • The Bible. We believe the Bible is God's Word and our guide for life.
  • Jesus Christ. We believe salvation comes by grace through faith in Jesus.
  • The Holy Spirit. We rely on the Holy Spirit to change and strengthen us.
  • The church family. We grow best together, in worship, prayer, and service.
